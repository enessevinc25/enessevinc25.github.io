What makes it different

Nothing dies if you miss a day

No decay, no sickness, no guilt screen. A missed day is bridged by a freeze you earn, or simply forgiven.

Streak recovery is never sold

You cannot buy your way out of a broken streak at any price. The pet is a readout of your days, not a slot machine.

No advertising SDK at all

Not “ads turned off” — the library is not in the app. No account, no server; your data stays on your device.

More than a checklist

Step-by-step routines, a focus timer with background sounds, breathing exercises, savings-style goals, a home-screen widget.
